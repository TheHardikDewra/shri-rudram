# Shri Rudram - Project Context

## What this is
Interactive website for learning and chanting Sri Rudram (Rudra Prashna / Shatarudriya) - the most revered Vedic hymn to Lord Shiva.

## Sacred Text - Handle With Utmost Care
- This is Vedic scripture from Krishna Yajurveda (Taittiriya Samhita, Kanda 4, Prapathaka 5)
- Ghanapathi-level Vedic chant with precise swaras (udatta, anudatta, svarita)
- Every Sanskrit syllable matters - never guess or fabricate
- Cross-verify all text against authoritative sources

## Structure
- **Namakam** - 11 anuvakas starting with "Namaste Rudra Manyava..."
  - Salutations to Rudra in all His forms
  - Each anuvaka ends with "Namo Namah" type refrain
- **Chamakam** - 11 anuvakas starting with "Agnavishnu Sajoshase..."
  - Prayers for divine blessings
  - Each anuvaka uses "cha me" (and may I be granted) refrain
- Total: 22 anuvakas

## Why Sri Rudram is chanted
- Considered the heart of the Yajurveda
- For grace of Mahayogi Shiva
- Singular most important hymn for atonement
- Central hymn in Rudra Abhishekam and Maha Rudra Yagna
- One of the oldest prayers in recorded history
